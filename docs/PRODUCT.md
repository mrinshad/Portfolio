- **Tone to use**: Professional, business-grounded, focused on reliability, security, and long-term maintainability.

### 2. Technical Hiring Teams & Engineering Leaders
- **Needs**: Verification of code quality, distributed system architecture, relational database schema design, and technical leadership capabilities.
- **Tone to use**: Concrete engineering terminology, architectural decisions, code craft, and clear attribution of individual impact.

3. **Database & Data Modeling**

5. **Technical Leadership & Systems Consulting**
   - System design documentation, architecture decision records (ADRs), and Git workflow governance.

## 5. Voice & Communication Guidelines

- **High Signal, Zero Fluff**: Avoid generic marketing jargon ("synergistic paradigm shift", "next-gen AI revolution"). State exactly what the system does, what technologies were selected, and what concrete problem was solved.
- **Authoritative & Measured**: Communicate with confidence rooted in hands-on production experience.
- **Attribution & Transparency**: Clearly articulate specific personal contributions, architecture trade-offs, and design rationales.
